  • Gilmore Girls
    • 2000
      Lauren Graham Alexis Bledel Melissa McCarthy

        In a small town called Stars Hollow, a young mom and her daughter who's bond is unbreakable experience love, parental issues, crazy neighbors, and lots of drama. I have recently finished this series and it is the most fustrating show I've ever watched. SLOWEST SLOWBURN EVER!! It takes the WHOLE SHOW for the main couple to get together even though its so obvious that they have feelings. Rory, the daughter, can get annoying with her love interests and choices. The characters can be fast to act and childish at times but you still tend to love them despite it. While a show can seem bad due to how fustrating it is, I find it fun, so much to rant about, but besides that it was a very fun, cozy, and comforting show, 7.5/10. I will soon watch Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life which seems like a promising follow-up on these girls lives. Gilmore Girls IMDb
